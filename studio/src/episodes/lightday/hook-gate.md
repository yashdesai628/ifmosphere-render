# Hook Gate — Lightday (`Lightday` Film & `LightdayShort` Vertical Short)

## 1. Opening Specification
- **First shot (Frame 0)**: Macro close-up of Pip's glowing phone in the dusk bedroom. Chat bubble `"hey 👋"` marked `SENT`. Live countdown timer beneath reads `"REPLY IN 48:00:00"`. Distant tiny spacecraft dot glinting through the bedroom window.
- **On-screen text (3–7 words)**: `REPLY IN 2 DAYS` (4 words, bold readable pill with sound off).
- **Spoken words (first line)**: `"You hit send. The reply comes back in two days."`
- **Payoff (next 2 lines, lock-in)**: `"Not because they're ignoring you. Because your friend is one light-day away, and on the eighteenth of November a real spacecraft gets there."` (Cut to Voyager 1 in deep space with `"18 NOV 2026"` on screen, before the title card `WHAT IF YOUR TEXT / TOOK A DAY?`).

## 2. Four-Failures Check
1. **Delay**: **PASS** — Frame 0 starts immediately inside the action on the phone screen (`"hey 👋"`, `SENT`, `"REPLY IN 48:00:00"`, `"REPLY IN 2 DAYS"`). Zero logo, greeting, or throat-clearing before the subject.
2. **Confusion**: **PASS** — Picture, on-screen text (`REPLY IN 2 DAYS`), and spoken first sentence (`"You hit send. The reply comes back in two days."`) say the exact same concrete thing in plain language.
3. **Irrelevance**: **PASS** — Uses second-person everyday action (`"You hit send"`) on a familiar phone chat UI that every viewer uses daily, contrasting instant texting with a 48-hour cosmic delay.
4. **Disinterest**: **PASS** — Immediate contrast hook (a normal `"hey 👋"` text taking 48 hours to get a reply) paired with a real upcoming milestone (`18 NOV 2026`, Voyager 1 reaching one light-day). No yes/no questions.

## 3. Viewer Panel (First 3 Seconds)
- **Doom-scroller (sound off)**: **STAY** — Sees the giant phone screen with `"hey 👋"`, `REPLY IN 48:00:00`, and bold `REPLY IN 2 DAYS` header immediately at frame 0.
- **Sceptic**: **STAY** — Line 2 answers the obvious objection (`"Not because they're ignoring you"`) and grounds it in a real NASA mission (`Voyager 1`, `18 NOV 2026`).
- **Beginner**: **STAY** — Understands texting delay instantly without needing physics jargon first.
- **Niche expert**: **STAY** — Recognises the real 18 November 2026 Voyager 1 one-light-day milestone (~16 billion miles / ~26 billion km).
- **Buyer / Curious learner**: **STAY** — Clear promise of a distance-delay ladder across the solar system and beyond.
- **Rival creator**: **STAY** — Fast multi-angle cuts, synced UI timer, and immediate visual payoff before the title card.
- **Panel tally**: **6 / 6 STAY** (Doom-scroller stays with sound off).

## 4. Lock-In Lines (1–3 sentences after hook)
> "Not because they're ignoring you. Because your friend is one light-day away, and on the eighteenth of November a real spacecraft gets there."

## 5. Visual-Match Check
- Frame 0 shows the subject: Pip's phone, message `"hey 👋"` marked `SENT`, reply timer `"REPLY IN 48:00:00"`.
- On-screen text: `"REPLY IN 2 DAYS"` (4 words, high-contrast readable with sound off).
- Spoken line: `"You hit send. The reply comes back in two days."`
- Title card (`WHAT IF YOUR TEXT / TOOK A DAY?`) appears only AFTER the Voyager 1 (`18 NOV 2026`) payoff.
