# Hook Gate — Closermoon (`Closermoon` Film & `ClosermoonShort` Vertical Short)

## 1. Opening Specification
- **First shot (Frame 0)**: A dark night beach under a colossal silver-gold Moon filling the sky above the ocean horizon, with tiny Pip standing on the wet sand looking up as moonlit surf glows at his feet. Distance readout pill shows `MOON: 192,200 KM` with a Moon-size gauge at `WIDTH x2 · AREA x4`.
- **On-screen text (3–7 words)**: `MOON 2X CLOSER` (3 words, high-contrast bold banner readable with sound off).
- **Spoken words (first line)**: `"The Moon just moved twice as close. Watch what the ocean does first."`
- **Payoff (next 2 lines, lock-in)**: `"A silver wall of water races up the sand right past your ankles, because the Moon's pull grows about eight times stronger when it gets twice as close. Cut its three hundred eighty-four thousand, four hundred kilometre distance in half to about one hundred ninety-two thousand, two hundred kilometres, and every shoreline on Earth feels that eightfold grip."` (Show `PULL x8` on screen as waves surge up the beach; title card `WHAT IF THE MOON / CAME 2X CLOSER?` comes only after the payoff).

## 2. Four-Failures Check
1. **Delay**: **PASS** — Frame 0 starts immediately on the night beach with the giant Moon filling the sky, tiny Pip on the sand, and bold `MOON 2X CLOSER` text. No logo, greeting, or title card before the subject.
2. **Confusion**: **PASS** — Visual (giant Moon over night beach + tiny Pip), on-screen text (`MOON 2X CLOSER`), and spoken line (`"The Moon just moved twice as close. Watch what the ocean does first."`) state the exact same concrete premise.
3. **Irrelevance**: **PASS** — Places "you" (Pip) directly on a familiar beach at night as the tide rushes past your ankles.
4. **Disinterest**: **PASS** — Immediate physical escalation (`Watch what the ocean does first` -> `PULL x8` inverse-cube tidal surge) with zero yes/no questions.

## 3. Viewer Panel (First 3 Seconds)
- **Doom-scroller (sound off)**: **STAY** — Giant Moon filling the sky above tiny Pip on the sand with bold `MOON 2X CLOSER` text reads in under half a second with sound off.
- **Sceptic**: **STAY** — Immediate quantitative payoff (`PULL x8` from inverse-cube tidal physics and NASA's `384,400 km` halved to `192,200 km`).
- **Beginner**: **STAY** — Instantly grasps the visual of the Moon moving twice as close and the ocean surging up the beach.
- **Niche expert**: **STAY** — Appreciates that tidal force scales with the inverse cube (`1/d^3`), so half the distance yields about `8x` tidal pull rather than `4x`.
- **Buyer / Curious learner**: **STAY** — Hook promises and immediately delivers concrete visual and scientific consequences.
- **Rival creator**: **STAY** — Fast multi-angle beach cuts, live distance readout (`384,400 km -> 192,200 km`), and `PULL x8` gauge before the title card.
- **Panel tally**: **6 / 6 STAY** (Doom-scroller stays with sound off).
